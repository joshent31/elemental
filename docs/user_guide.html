🏢 Department Workflows

1. Sales & Customer Service

Roles: Elemental Sales User / HOD
  • Create Elemental Quotation and send to customer.
  • On approval, click Create Job. Attach BOQ Excel and set start/due dates.
  • System automatically generates QR Masters, Design Tasks, QC Inspections, and Data Entry Tasks.

2. Design Department

Roles: Elemental Design User / HOD
  • Open auto-generated Design Task for each Finished Good.
  • Upload CAD drawings, record hours spent, and update status to Completed.

3. Data Entry Department

Roles: Elemental Data Entry User / HOD
  • Receive Job Data Entry Task.
  • Create Finished Good records and add FG Subparts with required processes (Cutting, Drilling, Welding, Painting, Assembly).
  • Link Finished Goods to Job table to trigger process QR generation.

4. Costing Department

Roles: Elemental Costing User / HOD
  • Click Pull Items from Job BOM on Job to auto-populate Material Indent.
  • System checks live stock; shortfalls auto-generate a Draft Purchase Order for Purchase.
  • Confirm Job Material Consumption after packaging completion.

5. Purchase Department

Roles: Elemental Purchase User / HOD
  • Review auto-created Draft Purchase Orders.
  • Select supplier, submit PO, and log Purchase Receipt on material delivery.

6. Production Department

Roles: Elemental Production User / HOD
  • Raise Material Issue to transfer stock to workshop floor as WIP.
  • Floor operators scan process QR codes at /scan-menu as parts move through Cutting, Drilling, Welding, Painting, and Assembly.
  • Intra-department transfers tracked via Department Transfer (/transfer-out & /transfer-in).

7. Quality Control (QC)

Roles: Elemental QC User / HOD
  • Scan QC Inspection QR code at /qc-scan.
  • Record Pass or Fail. Packaging is strictly blocked until QC status is Passed.

8. Packaging Department

Roles: Elemental Packaging User / HOD
  • Generate Packing Box labels.
  • Scan parts into boxes via /pack-box.
  • Mark Packaging Completed on Job once all FGs are packed to trigger material consumption accounting.

9. Dispatch Department

Roles: Elemental Dispatch User / HOD
  • Create Dispatch Entry with vehicle and driver details.
  • Scan boxes during vehicle loading at /dispatch-scan.
  • Mark Dispatched and Delivered to close the Job.
